- Free-tier-first doctrine (roadmap order: free tier → usage-based/no-minimum → low fixed cost → paid only where required): record each chosen provider, its CURRENT limits/terms as operational constraints, and the degradation/fallback story. - Rollback/repoint: config-driven (domain authority unchanged when a deployment is rolled back or repointed) — a drill-style test proves it locally.
- Secret isolation: env/secret references are named, typed and scanned (your battery includes a secret scan over deploy surfaces); credential isolation between environments is asserted by tests, not prose.
