## 3. What you are building (the shape)

A benchmark module + runner that, for EVERY corpus pair, executes BOTH
authoring paths against the SAME deterministic baseline scene and compares
the CANONICAL results:

```
             ┌─ NL path: compile({utterance, session}) ──→ intent(agent origin)
corpus pair ─┤                                                    │
             └─ DM path: createOperationIntent(direct input) ─────┤
                                                                  ▼
                    materializeBaselineState(scene) — ONE baseline per pair
                                                                  ▼
              applyOperation(baseline, intent) ──→ applied | refused
              validateSolutionVersion(...)      ──→ verdict
              BOQ resolution                    ──→ delta lines
                                                                  ▼
              project BOTH results onto PROD-029's canonical points and
              compare: EQUIVALENT or DECLARED-DIFFERENT(kind)
```

The four behavior-matrix cells (closed vocabulary — `EquivalenceExpectation`):
- `equivalent` — both paths produce intents; canonical results identical
  (identity semantics, post-state digest, validation verdict, BOQ delta).
- `declared-different` — both paths produce intents; the results differ and
  the corpus entry DECLARES the expected difference kind (e.g. the NL
  replacement clause "instead of 30 mm" carries removed-old-value semantics
  the direct twin deliberately omits — a parameter-semantics difference).
- `agent-refused` — the NL utterance trips the unsafe taxonomy
  (authority claim / determinism bypass): NO intent on the agent path. The
  benchmark records the refusal reason as the DESIGNED outcome; the direct
  path may still author (and the engine governs both equally).
- `agent-clarification` — the NL utterance is missing a required slot:
  `clarification-needed` with the targeted question. Same recording rule.
