## Truth laws (any violation = lead rejection at merge)

- Authorized APIs and official exports/user-supplied files ONLY. No scraping, no bypass of access controls, CAPTCHAs, anti-bot/rate limits, DRM, or hidden private endpoints.
- Source-native feed order must NEVER be labeled as WebFlix-ranked content.
- Snapshot must NEVER be presented as live.
- Imported records retain provenance and survive source disconnection unless the user explicitly deletes them.
- Import must be idempotent (re-import = no duplicates).
- Production connectors must not silently fall back to fixtures.
- Repository is the source of truth; you may not change shared contracts beyond your lane's additions without noting it in your report for lead ratification.
