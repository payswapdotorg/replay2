## 3. What you are building (the shape)

A provider-neutral VLM reasoning benchmark lane with Qwen3-VL as the
registered candidate family, fully deterministic:

1. **Provider profiles**: register Qwen3-VL 8B and Qwen3-VL 30B-A3B as TWO
   SEPARATE profiles (distinct provider ids/versions, same family) through the
   control plane — mandatory profile fields complete, modality/capability
   declarations covering image, video, OCR and spatial-reference tasks, cost
   model + latency/resource profile metadata (`COST_MODELS`), license
   declaration `evaluation-only`. Both profiles enter the append-only registry
   via lawful events; their benchmark records are comparable
   (`benchmarkComparabilityKey`) so a future real-model run slots in without
   schema change.
2. **The corpus** (deterministic fixtures, in-repo): multimodal reasoning
   scenarios over evidence bundles — image evidence (fixtures describing
   photos/renders of building elements), video evidence (frame sequences as
   structured fixtures), OCR evidence (text regions with coordinates),
   spatial-reference questions ("the beam above the door on the north
   elevation" style references that must resolve through the bundle, not
   world knowledge). Each scenario declares its evidence bundle WITH REVISION
   IDS; the expected answer must bind to those revisions.
3. **Fixture doubles** for the model: deterministic in-repo stand-ins (the
   `providerFixture`/testkit pattern) with THREE behavior classes —
   well-grounded (answers bound to bundle evidence, correct envelope),
   hallucinating (asserts a measurement/material/observation ABSENT from the
   bundle — must be caught), and refusing (missing/ambiguous evidence →
   clarification or bounded refusal with the right envelope status). The
   double's behavior is data-driven per scenario.
4. **The behavior matrix** (mandatory, tested): unsupported-question (out of
   the declared capability set → explicit unsupported, never a guess);
   missing-evidence (the bundle lacks what the question needs →
   clarification/bounded refusal, NEVER an invented measurement);
   conflicting-evidence (two bundle items disagree → surfaced conflict, no
   silent resolution); grounded-pass (the correct envelope bound to the right
   revisions).
5. **Grounded-reasoning checks**: every well-grounded answer's envelope is
   validated (`parseDeclaredEvidenceEnvelope` + integrity rules) AND its
   facts are cross-checked against the bundle by DETERMINISTIC checks —
  provider-claimed facts not derivable from the bundle are failures
   (unsupported-data). Deterministic checks remain authoritative: the harness
   recomputes what is recomputable and compares.
